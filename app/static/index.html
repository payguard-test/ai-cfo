The period's ledger and statements are fingerprinted (SHA-256) and the fingerprints are written to a NEAR contract. No amounts or names go on chain. Anyone shown these books can check they were not changed afterwards.

How the agent works

1. Read the bank alert and extract amount, direction, date, counterparty, reference.
2. Mask account numbers, then classify privately on NEAR AI Cloud (TEE). Save the signed proof.
3. Post balanced double-entry lines. Low confidence goes to review instead of guessing.
4. Notify Telegram. Build P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and capital position.
5. Anchor the period's fingerprints on NEAR testnet.
