# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

1. Problem (10 s): small businesses get a bank alert for every naira, but nobody keeps the books.
2. Dashboard, click "Send all samples" (20 s): 19 alerts become classified transactions. Point at owner capital and the loan: money in, but not sales.
3. Telegram (15 s): show the messages arriving on the phone, then type /pnl.
4. Review (15 s): the vague "Transfer" from John Doe is flagged, not guessed. Set it to Sales revenue; revenue and "To review" update.
5. Privacy (15 s): open a transaction, show "Private inference proof: verified" and the TEE signing address.
6. Statements (15 s): Profit and loss, Balance sheet "Balances: Yes", Cash flow.
7. NEAR (25 s): Anchor this period, open the testnet link, Verify: match. Change one category, Verify again: books changed.
8. Close (5 s): link to the live demo and repo.
